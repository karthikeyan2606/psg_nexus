About PSG College of Technology

Institutional overview and general information.

Introduction

PSG College of Technology is an engineering and technology institution located in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

The institution provides education in engineering, technology, science and management-related disciplines.

Location

PSG College of Technology is located in the Peelamedu area of Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

Academic Environment

The institution offers undergraduate and postgraduate education across various technical and professional disciplines. Students can explore programme information through the academic programmes page.
